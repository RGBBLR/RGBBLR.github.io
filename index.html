Professional Summary

I've spent over a decade on the planning side of real estate development in Bengaluru, with RMZ, Prestige, Godrej Properties and now Ramsons Trendsquares. The work has been schedules, budgets and capital deployment on offices, malls, high-rise housing and mixed-use projects.

At Ramsons I plan how capital goes into one mixed-use development across residential, retail, commercial and hotel. Separately, I run my own research on Bengaluru supply: land and approvals, upcoming launches, and how fast each micro-market is selling. I build it from government records, not news reports.

So I can look at a project two ways. Will its cost and schedule hold on site, and will the market pay the price the plan needs? That's the view I offer investors putting money into Bengaluru development.
