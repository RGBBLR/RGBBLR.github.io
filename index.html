Professional Summary

I've spent over a decade on the planning side of real estate development in Bengaluru, with RMZ, Prestige, Godrej Properties and now Ramsons Trendsquares. The work has been schedules, budgets and capital deployment on offices, malls, high-rise housing and mixed-use projects.

At Ramsons I plan how capital goes into one mixed-use development across residential, retail, commercial and hotel. Separately, I run BREI (Bengaluru Real Estate Insider), an independent research and advisory practice. It tracks Bengaluru supply before it reaches the market: land and approvals, upcoming launches, and how fast each micro-market is selling. I build it from government records, not news reports. The pipeline now covers 530+ projects and 5,600+ acres of pre-launch land.

So I can look at a project two ways. Will its cost and schedule hold on site, and will the market pay the price the plan needs? That's the view I offer investors putting money into Bengaluru development, and the basis of BREI's work for developers, landowners and capital partners.

Where I Fit With Capital Partners
Pre-Investment Diligence
Before money goes in, I test the developer's cost, schedule and CAPEX phasing against what contractors charge and how long the work really takes.
Deployment Monitoring
Once money is in, I track what's been drawn against what's been built, how the contractor is holding up financially, and the approval and RERA milestones the next tranche depends on.
Market & Supply Read
I keep an independent view of Bengaluru micro-markets: what's launching nearby, what's coming through approvals, and what's selling at what price. The live version is the BREI pipeline.
Expansion Planning
On the developer side, I work out what to build and in what sequence, and make sure cash flow across segments keeps pace with the site.
